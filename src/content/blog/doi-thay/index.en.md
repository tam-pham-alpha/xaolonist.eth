This morning, an old tree on Đinh Tiên Hoàng Street suddenly came out of the ground. No heavy rain, no storm, a tree that had stood there through so many years now lay down in the middle of a very ordinary morning. He looked at the photo for quite a while, with a feeling in his chest that was hard to name

In the way the old people saw the world, a tree belongs to Wood. Wood is growth, the things that sink their roots into the earth and then quietly reach for the sky. We are also in the early years of Period 9, a cycle of Fire, where everything moves faster, shines brighter, and the old structures seem easier to expose

Wood gives birth to Fire. A fallen tree is not necessarily a loss, sometimes it is only changing from one form of energy into another. What has gathered for many years can become fuel for a new cycle, the way an old forest, after a season of fire, begins to put out other shoots

He does not think this is a bad omen. He only sees it as a reminder of a turning. There are things that seemed to have rooted themselves very deep, that seemed ready to stand for another hundred years, but under the ground, the change may have already begun a long time ago

And then on a morning with no storm at all, the old thing simply leaves. A turning of the cycle may often happen this way, very gently, until the moment we suddenly notice that the scenery around us has already become different
