There are mornings when he sits with coffee, the phone lying still on the table, and nothing special happens. Once that was just an ordinary morning. Now he finds moments like that increasingly precious

AI is helping people create a volume of information the world has never seen. An essay takes a few seconds. An image takes a few words. Video, music, stories, advice, knowledge, opinions, all of it is being born much faster than any one person can receive. We once lived in a world where information was scarce, so we had to go looking for it. It seems we are stepping into the opposite world, where information comes looking for us

And when information becomes nearly infinite, what grows scarce is the empty space between those pieces of information

A stretch of time with no notifications. A meal that does not need a screen. An afternoon walk without a podcast. A conversation that does not need to know what is happening out there. Or simply a few minutes of sitting still, learning nothing extra, not trying to become a better version of himself

He once thought the new era would reward those who knew the most. Now he is less sure. When anyone can ask AI and receive an answer in a few seconds, knowing one more thing may no longer be as hard as before. Harder is knowing what deserves his attention, what should be allowed to pass, and when everything should be closed

Perhaps peace in the age of AI does not come from finding answers to everything. It comes from the ability to live with a few questions without immediately opening the phone to hunt for a reply

The world ahead will probably never grow quieter. Machines will keep writing, speaking, singing, and making new things even while we sleep. Only each person's attention remains as finite as it was a thousand years ago

And who knows, in a world that can create nearly everything, the greatest luxury may simply be an afternoon where nothing happens
